What We Do

Five pillars. One trusted partner.

From first workload to full sovereignty, Axorvault Systems covers the full architecture lifecycle — cloud, data, security and governance — for institutions that cannot fail.

Cloud Architecture

Landing zones, multi-cloud and sovereign cloud platforms engineered for resilience — with IaC, DevSecOps and FinOps baked in from day one.

  • Azure, AWS & GCP landing zones
  • Infrastructure as Code & CI/CD
  • Resilience & disaster recovery

Data Architecture

Modern data platforms engineered for trust — lakehouse foundations, pipelines, mesh patterns and analytics that are lineage-clear, cost-aware and ready for governance from the start.

  • Lakehouse & data mesh patterns
  • Pipelines, ETL/ELT & orchestration
  • Analytics, BI & data products

Cybersecurity Architecture

Zero-trust architectures, identity engineering, segmentation and detection capability — security designed into the blueprint, not bolted on after.

  • Zero-trust & identity architecture
  • Network segmentation & micro-VM
  • Detection, response & audit evidence

Data Governance

End-to-end stewardship of your data estate — catalogues, lineage, quality, privacy and retention policies that turn data into accountable public value.

  • Data cataloguing & lineage
  • PII / privacy controls (PIPA, MIA)
  • Retention & classification frameworks

Public Sector Trusted

Deep fluency in government operating models — clearances, procurement, accessibility, and the sovereignty requirements modern public services demand.

  • Clearance-ready delivery teams
